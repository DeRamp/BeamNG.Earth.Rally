Earth Rally · stats and reports (proposal)

Help improve Earth Rally?

Earth Rally can send a few anonymous counts, like which stages you run and whether you finish them. It shows which stages people drive and what needs fixing.

What it sends

  • Mod and game version
  • Stages and rallies started and finished
  • Stage time, resets, retirements
  • A random install number

Never sent

  • Your name, Steam or BeamNG account
  • Your IP address or location
  • Your other mods or your PC
  • Logs, unless you send a report

Change your mind any time in Settings › Privacy. Full details, from DeRamp: github.com/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/PRIVACY.md
