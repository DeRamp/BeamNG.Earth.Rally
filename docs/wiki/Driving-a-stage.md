# Driving a stage

Press **Drive stage**. The mod runs it like a real event.

1. **Time control.** You are set down just before the arrival time control (the red clock board). Stop at it to get
   your start time on the rally clock. Driving past it costs 60 s.
2. **Start line.** Roll up and stop on the line. The start tree and the co-driver count you down. Early? **Shift + E**
   skips to 6 s before your start. A jump start costs 10 s.
3. **The stage.** Stage clock, distance done and pacenote cards are on screen. Leave the line and an **OFF ROUTE**
   card shows the way back. Each reset costs 10 s.
4. **Flying finish.** The clock stops there. Drive on to the stop control for your results card.
