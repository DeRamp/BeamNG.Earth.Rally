# Stage dressing

Stages are dressed like a real event, from the game's own rally props.

* **Course tape** across every side road the stage doesn't take.
* **Direction boards** before junction turns and forks.
* **Junction marshals** behind the tape, on the closed road furthest from a car that misses the turn.
* **Radio and medical points** with warning boards.
* **Spectator areas** at hairpins and big turns.

## Where the points come from

Each stage page shows, per kind, **on the map**, **Random** or **None**:

* **On the map**: the organisers marked them; they go where the map puts them.
* **Random**: the rally's map marks none of that kind, so they are placed where the stage has room.
* **None**: the map marks that kind on other stages but not this one. Medical points only go where marked.

![Map points on a stage page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/images/menu-stage-map-points.jpg)

## Good to know

* Dressing comes from **Auto recce** only. Stages recced before 1.6.0 get direction boards until you **Reset** and
  run **Auto recce** again.
* Props can still land on a road the game's map doesn't have, such as a driveway it is missing.
* How to mark points in your own map: [map tags](https://github.com/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/blob/main/docs/rally-file.md#marking-the-organisers-points-in-a-map).
