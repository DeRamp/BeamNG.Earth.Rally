# Rally packs and leaderboards

The menu goes **Rallies > season > rally > stage**. A single map you drop in is a rally of its own. A **rally pack**
carries a whole season.

![A season](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/images/menu-season.jpg)

## What a pack can bring

* **Covers and logos** for the menu.
* **The itinerary**: a stage run twice shows as one row, *SS2 · SS5*.
* **Real results**: every stage gets a leaderboard of the real crews' times.
* **Rules**: penalties for a false start, and for arriving late or early at a control.
* **Season standings**, from each rally's finishing order (25, 20, 16, 13, 11 ... points).

A pack is a folder, or a `.zip` of one, in the import folder, with a `season.json`, `rally.json` or `stage.json` at
its top. Everything in it is optional. Format: [`docs/rally-file.md`](https://github.com/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/blob/main/docs/rally-file.md).

## Where you place

* A stage's **Leaderboard** shows the top 10, then the crew just ahead of you, you, and the crew just behind.
* A rally's **Results** tab adds your best on each stage into a rally total, once every stage has a time.
* The season's **Standings** tab ranks you with the real crews.
* Your time is placed only when your line is within 5 % of the official distance. Otherwise it stays under
  **Your runs**, marked *not placed*.
