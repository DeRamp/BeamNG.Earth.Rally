# Your first stage

From an empty menu to a stage time.

1. **Add a rally.** Press **Ctrl + Alt + K**, then **Open folder** at the bottom of Rallies. Drop in a rally's map
   (`.kml`, `.kmz`, `.gpx`) or a rally pack. It imports straight away.
2. **Open it.** Pick the rally, then a stage.
3. **Give the stage a line.** Press **Auto recce**. It checks the map data, asks before downloading anything, takes
   you to the start and works the line out from the roads. See [[Auto recce]].
4. **Drive stage.** You are taken to the time control before the start. See [[Driving a stage]].

![A rally's stages](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/images/menu-rally.jpg)

## What the stage list tells you

* **Ready**: the stage has a line; pacenotes follow the road all the way.
* **Missing road segments**: part of the stage has no road on the map, so no notes there until you drive a recce.
* **Recce stops short**: your own recce ends before the finish. Drive it again.
* **Recce needed**: no line yet. Press **Auto recce**, or **Recce** to drive it once yourself.
* **Best** and **Place**: your best time, and where it ranks against the real crews.
