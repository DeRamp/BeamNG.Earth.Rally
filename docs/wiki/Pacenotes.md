# Pacenotes

The game's own co-driver reads pacenotes worked out from the stage's line, and the cards show them on screen.

![Pacenote cards on the stage](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wiki/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/images/hero-on-stage.jpg)

## What is called

* **Corners** with their number and length: *three left long*.
* **Hairpin**, **square**, **chicane**, long **sweepers**.
* **Tightens** / **opens**, and a **caution** where a corner comes faster than it looks.
* **Crests** where the driver can't see the road ahead, not wherever the grade changes. *Down* at the top of a
  downhill from flat road.
* **Jump**, **dip**, **up** / **down** from the heights.
* With **Auto recce**: **junctions** (*at junction*, *keep left*) and **over bridge**.
* **Over finish** near the flying finish.

A casual co-driver voice hears only the calls it can say. Every call and the figures behind it:
[`docs/pacenotes-1.5.0.md`](https://github.com/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/blob/main/docs/pacenotes-1.5.0.md).

## Good to know

* Where the map has no road, the organisers' line is used and nothing is called there.
* Very broad hilltops are not called as crests.
