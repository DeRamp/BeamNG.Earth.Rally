# Auto recce

**Auto recce** works out a stage's driving line from BeamNG.Earth's roads, so you never have to drive a recce.

1. Open a stage and press **Auto recce**.
2. If map data is missing along the stage, it says how much and asks. **Not now** stops with nothing downloaded
   and nobody moved.
3. It takes you to the start and matches the line, 700 m at a time. You can leave the page while it works.
4. The stage turns **Ready**, with its corners, climb and drop. Where the map has no road, it says a recce
   would be better.

## Good to know

* It needs [the DeRamp fork of BeamNG.Earth](https://github.com/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth/releases). On official
  BeamNG.Earth the button is greyed out, with a note under it; drive a **Recce** instead.
* Junction calls, bridges and [[Stage dressing]] come from Auto recce only.
* Once a stage has a line, **Auto recce** becomes **Reset**. Reset removes the line; your times stay.
* **Recce** instead: drive the stage once at your own pace. The orange line on the road leads to the flying finish
  (the green ball), and the line you drove is saved there.
