# Controls and settings

| What | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| **Open / close the menu** | **Ctrl + Alt + K** (**Esc** closes) | bind one, or the pause menu's **Rally menu** |
| Skip the wait on the start line | **Shift + E** | modifier + **Y** |
| Reset the car (+10 s on a stage) | **R** / **Insert** | the game's own |
| **Back on the road** where you got to (+10 s) | **Backspace** | bind one |
| **Flip upright** where you are (no penalty) | **Shift + Backspace** | the radial menu |

To rebind: **Options > Controls > Bindings**, category **Gameplay**, the actions starting **Earth Rally:**.

## Settings

**Settings**, top right of the menu:

* **Units**: km or miles.
* **Reset penalty**: +10 s per reset on a stage, on or off.
* **Control-zone brake**: the brakes ease in at a control, so you stop at its board.
* **Start clock view**: where the start clock's close-up shows during the countdown.
