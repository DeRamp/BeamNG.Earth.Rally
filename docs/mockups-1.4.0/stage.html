Earth Rally stage dressing

1.4.0 mock-up, seen from above at true scale. Not built.

Each drawing is a stretch of road seen from above, north up, and the car comes in from the bottom. The yellow wash is the stage's line. Everything else is a stock BeamNG model from the game's rally kit, placed by the rules in the plan. No people: the game has no human models, so a crowd or a marshal is implied by tape, chairs, tables, a gazebo and a flag.

Legend

Sizes are estimates for every board and post. The tape lengths are the models' own (5, 6, 8, 10 m) and the gazebo is the 3×3 m model props.lua already uses at the controls. Real footprints get read from the models at build time.

7. A whole stage (schematic, 20 km)

Where each kind lands along one stage, for a map that marks nothing (LSPR, OFR, SnoDrift, OTR) and for one that does (Overmountain-style). Filled marks came from the organisers' map; outlined ones were placed by rule. The bottom row shows what is in the world at one moment.

  • Radio: every ~5 km when the map marks none, never in the last 1 km. A map's own radio points replace the rule for that stage.
  • Spectators: at most one per ~4 km, at the tightest hairpins and biggest junction turns, none in the first 500 m.
  • Marshals: at turns at junctions, at most one per ~1 km. Medical: only where the map marks it.
  • Tape at every junction's untaken roads, and boards at every turn and fork, from either source.
  • The rule is applied per kind: a map that marks marshals but no radio points still gets automatic radio points.
