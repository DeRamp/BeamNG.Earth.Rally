One control, one slot, two labels. Auto recce on a stage with no line; Reset on a stage that has one. Reset removes the line, the stage goes back to Recce needed, and from there the player drives a recce or presses Auto recce — the two paths that already exist.

1Today: the action cell is empty on every row that has a line

This is the dead end, drawn rather than described. The 160px action column was added in 1.3.0 and is used by exactly one branch — st.autoOk && s.status === 'none'. The moment a line exists the cell is blank and there is no way back short of deleting a file by hand.

The same four rows. Every row now has exactly one control, and the column finally reads as one thing: what can I do about this stage's line?

Why Reset is outlined and Auto recce stays secondary. The game's accents already carry this distinction and it is worth using rather than inventing: secondary is the filled weight the row uses for the thing you probably want, outlined the quieter one it uses for Stop. A destructive action should not be the loudest thing in the row, and Reset is the only destructive control in this UI.

Why not a seventh state on the pill, or a second control in Status

Both were drawn and dropped in 1.3.0's own mock-up and the arithmetic has not changed: Status is 260px and already holds the longest pill (Recce recommended, about 177px) with its 30px padding, so it cannot take a control as well. And Reset is not a state of the stage — it is something the player does to it, which is what the action column is for.

3The stage page

The same substitution, and it needs no new condition: the Auto recce button is v-if="stg.autoCan", and menu.lua:139 sets autoCan = st.status == 'none' or nil — so on a stage with a line it is already absent. Reset is its v-else-if.

Confirming the cheap case as well would be worse than not confirming at all: it teaches the habit of clicking through the dialog, and the one time it mattered the player would click through that too. The data to tell them apart is already there — stg.line is st.kind, 'matched' or 'recce' (menu.lua:133).

Reset Founders, Olmstead, Moore, Nagle Super Special?

This removes the recce you drove. There is no way to get it back, and the auto recce cannot make one here — the map has no roads for this stage on your computer.

your recce: 5.7 km, 1400 points, driven 14 Nov 01:08

The dialog names what is lost and what can replace it. On this stage nothing can — it is one of the 82 stages with no cached roads — and saying so is the difference between a confirm and a formality. The buttons say what they do rather than Yes and No.

The second sentence is conditional, and worth the trouble. Where the auto recce can run it reads “the auto recce can make one from the map's roads in a couple of seconds” — which is the case where Reset is harmless. Where it cannot, the player is told before they press, not after.

5What Reset removes: both files, not one

Read in events.lua:137 rather than assumed. A stage can have both lines on disk at once, and stageLine picks between them — the driven recce wins unless it is short (share < 0.9, or stopping RECCE_SHORT_OF_FF_M before the flying finish) and a matched line exists.

on diskremove only the one in useremove both (proposed)
matched only→ Recce needed ✓→ Recce needed ✓
recce only→ Recce needed ✓→ Recce needed ✓
both→ still has a line ✗→ Recce needed ✓

In the third row, removing the driven recce would leave the matched line to answer stageLine, the stage would stay Ready or Recce recommended, and the player would have pressed Reset and watched nothing happen. Reset removes both, so the word means what it says: the stage is as it was before either line existed.

This is also why the confirm asks about the driven recce specifically. Where both are present the dialog names both, and the driven one is the reason it is being asked at all.

The last row is the one to check. Reset removes files and asks BeamNG.Earth for nothing, so it must not be gated on st.autoOk the way Auto recce is. A player on a stock BeamNG.Earth who drove a recce they are unhappy with still gets their way back. Gating both on the same flag would be the easy mistake here, and it would lock exactly the players who have no auto recce to fall back on.

What this leaves open for the owner

  • The confirm's wording above is a first draft. The shape is what matters: name what is lost, say whether anything can replace it, and label the buttons with their actions.
  • Does Reset also clear the stage's times? Drawn as no — results/ is a record of what the player drove and a line is not part of it. Worth a word, since "reset" could be read either way.
