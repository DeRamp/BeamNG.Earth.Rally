EARTH RALLY - TEMPLATES

Three ways to share rallies, one folder each. Copy the one you want, fill it in, and put it in
Earth Rally's import folder (in game: Rally menu > Add > Open folder).

  My Stage    one stage on its own
  My Rally    a rally: its stages in running order, services, days, results, weather, rules
  My Season   several rallies with championship standings

Each folder needs your stage map (.kml, .kmz or .gpx) next to its .txt file. Draw it in
Google My Maps or anything that saves KML: one line per stage, in the driving direction,
named after the stage.

Every .txt and .csv file explains itself: open it in Notepad (or Excel for .csv) and follow
the notes. Mistakes are fine: each time the game reads a folder it writes "import report.txt"
in it, saying what it found and what to fix.

More: https://github.com/DeRamp/BeamNG.Earth.Rally/blob/main/docs/making-rally-packs.md
